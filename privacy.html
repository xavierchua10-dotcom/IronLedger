Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Plain-language summary from the app's independent developer, not a law firm — written to actually be readable rather than as dense legal boilerplate.

1. What we collect

If you use Guest mode, nothing leaves your device — no account, no data collection. If you create an account, we store:

  • Your email address (for login)
  • Your workout data — sessions, sets, routines, bodyweight, training goals, and app preferences — so it syncs across your devices
  • Basic subscription status (Pro/trial/free), managed through Stripe

2. Who we share it with

We don't sell your data or share it with advertisers. A few services help run the app under the hood:

ServiceWhat it handles
Firebase (Google)Account login, and storing/syncing your workout data
StripePayment processing — we never see or store your card details
NetlifyRuns backend functions for payments and notifications

3. Push notifications

If you enable notifications, we use Firebase Cloud Messaging to send an occasional nudge (like an inactivity reminder). You can disable this anytime in Settings.

4. Your rights

From Settings, at any time, you can:

  • Export a full JSON backup of your data
  • Delete your account and all associated data permanently

5. Data security

Your data is stored in Firebase's Firestore database with access rules restricting it to your own account. No system is 100% immune to risk, but standard industry security practices are followed.

6. Children's privacy

SaviorFit Logger isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes meaningfully, the "last updated" date above will reflect it.
